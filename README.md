I lead technology at **Shieldwise**, building an educational ecosystem that helps organizations train people against real, modern threats. Alongside that I teach **Cryptography** and **Introduction to Cybersecurity** at university level, and research privacy, data protection and AI-driven risks.

## 🛡️ Shieldwise — Cybersecurity Training & Education

As CTO, I own the technical roadmap for our learning platform and curriculum:

- **Learning platform** — a scalable architecture that delivers seamless, localized training, plus the simulation engines behind it.
- **Compliance curriculum** — technical modules for **GDPR**, **ISO 27001** and **DORA**.
- **Modern risks** — training people to spot **deepfakes**, **AI voice cloning** and today's scams.
- **Measuring resilience** — the **Security Resilience Score (SRS)**, a human-centric security metric from my M.Sc. research on cognitive threat modeling.
